# Wedding Planning Queue

The high-level interview is complete. Keep this queue actionable, dependency-aware, and synchronized with PROJECT.md and DECISIONS.md.

## P0 — Start now

### Destination selection

- [ ] Build evaluation criteria and weights from the recorded requirements.
- [ ] Create a 3–5 destination shortlist; include Armenia and Turkey but do not assume either wins.
- [ ] Verify current entry rules for Iranian and Italian passports, overland route practicality from Karaj, flight access, Sep–Dec weather, safety/reliability, venue ecosystem, planner availability, and indicative costs.
- [ ] Recommend which destinations advance to market testing.
- Dependency: none.
- Decision gate: destination country/region after budget and access evidence.

### Guest list and attendance model

- [ ] Choose the guest-list owner and working format.
- [ ] Create named households with country, passport/travel considerations, children, priority tier, and attendance confidence.
- [ ] Produce low/base/high attendance scenarios without deliberately cutting desired invitees.
- Dependency: couple supplies or drafts names.
- Feeds: budget, venue capacity, transport, hotel guidance, RSVP design.

### Budget feasibility

- [ ] Build an initial category budget for low/base/high attendance scenarios.
- [ ] Keep rings and four-room family hotel week visible as separate lines.
- [ ] Add taxes, transport, planner, weather backup, currency risk, and contingency.
- [ ] Identify the first price evidence needed to determine whether €30k is realistic.
- Dependency: current requirements are sufficient to begin; refine with guest model and destination quotes.

### Planner-ready requirements brief

- [ ] Convert PROJECT.md and MEMORY.md into a concise outreach brief and comparable quote questionnaire.
- [ ] Require all-in pricing, inclusions/exclusions, taxes, minimum/final headcount terms, payment/cancellation terms, and Plan B details.
- Dependency: draft now; localize after destination shortlist.

## P1 — Begin when initial destination shortlist exists

- [ ] Identify and screen full-service planners in each advancing destination.
- [ ] Build venue archetype/region shortlist and indicative package comparisons.
- [ ] Compare planner/venue/date combinations on total cost and execution risk.
- [ ] Check family hotel and guest transport practicality.
- [ ] Maintain a risk register for border/flight disruption, weather, currency, contracts, and remote execution.
- [ ] Decide destination country/region, then planner, venue, and date.

## P2 — After destination/date/venue are secured

- [ ] Contract core suppliers: catering/bar if separate, DJ, photographer, transport, and essential rentals.
- [ ] Secure the four-room family hotel stay and seek no-commitment guest rates/codes.
- [ ] Send digital save-the-date and launch basic travel-information website.
- [ ] Select wedding rings within an explicit allocation.
- [ ] Develop visual identity from the venue.
- [ ] Run traditions workshop; choose ceremony structure, vows, roles, and ring presenter.
- [ ] Design menu/bar, music, cake, decor/florals, invitations, and RSVP.

## P3 — RSVP and operational planning

- [ ] Collect attendance, children, diet, travel, accommodation, and shuttle information.
- [ ] Build seating plan and venue-ready layout.
- [ ] Finalize speeches, programme, timeline, transport, weather trigger, vendor contacts, payment schedule, and day-of responsibilities.
- [ ] Confirm final headcount as late as contracts allow.

## P4 — Final month and closeout

- [ ] Final vendor confirmations, fittings/beauty trials, rehearsal/briefing, packing, rings, family transfers, emergency kit, and final payments.
- [ ] Execute wedding-week plan and complete post-event returns, balances, thank-yous, and artifact archive.

3. Should the €30k feasibility model treat that figure as a firm ceiling, a target with modest flexibility, or an initial hypothesis?
4. Are there any destinations besides Armenia and Turkey the couple wants included or excluded before research?
5. How involved does the couple want to be in planner/vendor calls and negotiations?
