# Wedding Project Brief

## Objective

Plan and execute a wedding in Turkey for a couple who lives outside Turkey, using this repository as the central planning workspace and the assistant as a proactive wedding-planning copilot.

## Current phase

Discovery / requirements gathering.

The project should not jump prematurely into venue or vendor selection before enough context is known to judge fit. Research can proceed in parallel where useful, especially for wedding planners who can support remote planning.

## Known constraints

- Destination: Turkey.
- Planning is being done from abroad.
- Local execution support will probably be required.
- The couple does not want to rely on already knowing the full wedding-planning process; the assistant should identify workstreams proactively.

## Working assumptions

These are provisional and must be validated:

- Hiring a Turkey-based full-service planner or strong local coordinator will likely reduce execution risk.
- City/region, season/date, guest count, and budget will be the highest-leverage early decisions because they constrain most later choices.
- Cultural/language fit may matter when selecting a planner and vendors, given the existing interest in Iranian/Persian wedding planning in Turkey.

## Success criteria

The project should ultimately produce:

- a clear wedding vision and priority hierarchy;
- a realistic budget and contingency;
- a confirmed date and location;
- a venue and appropriate planner/coordinator;
- reliable vendor team and signed contracts;
- guest travel/accommodation/communication plan;
- culturally appropriate ceremony and celebration plan;
- complete logistics and wedding-day timeline;
- tracked payment, deadline, and decision schedule;
- backup plans for material risks;
- a low-friction planning process despite the couple living abroad.

## Primary discovery questions

The initial interview needs to establish:

1. The emotional and experiential vision for the wedding.
2. The couple's top priorities and deal-breakers.
3. Approximate guest count and where guests will travel from.
4. Desired date, season, flexibility, and any meaningful dates to avoid/prefer.
5. Budget posture and what the budget should include.
6. Preferred parts of Turkey, if any, and desired setting.
7. Cultural, religious, legal, family, and language requirements.
8. Desired level of planner involvement.
9. Expected surrounding events and guest experience.
10. Biggest concerns or uncertainties about planning remotely.

## Major workstreams

The precise plan will be generated after discovery, but likely workstreams include:

- planning & governance;
- budget;
- planner selection;
- destination/venue/date;
- guest list and guest logistics;
- ceremony and cultural traditions;
- design/decor/florals;
- food and beverage;
- entertainment;
- photo/video;
- attire/beauty;
- invitations/RSVP/seating;
- travel/accommodation/transportation;
- vendor contracts/payments;
- wedding-week events;
- wedding-day operations and contingency planning.
